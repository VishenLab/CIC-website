Cellular Interface Cluster

Understanding cellular interface at all scales of biological organisation.

A multidisciplinary alliance advancing membrane interfaces, mechanobiology, imaging, and bioelectric tissue science.

Research Themes

How does membrane organisation control the cell’s ability to sense and respond to its environment?

This question underlies the Mayor group’s exploration of membrane composition, nanoclusters, and non-clathrin endocytic pathways in living cells.

How do cortical forces and membrane tension coordinate to direct cell motility and cell–cell contact?

The Köster group asks how the plasma membrane and actin cortex feedback to regulate mechanical properties and signalling at the cellular interface.

How can fluorescence imaging reveal the rules of nanoscale molecular motion in membranes and tissues?

Schneider’s lab develops high-sensitivity optical methods to pose this question across synthetic membranes, cells, and zebrafish embryos.

How do neurons sense tissue stiffness, and how does this influence electrical maturation?

Kreysing investigates the mechanical cues that shape neuronal development, using plasmonic and biophysical probes to answer this challenge.

How do bioelectric signals and mechanics together organise developing tissue form?

Singh’s group frames morphogenesis as a coupled electro-mechanical problem, seeking the principles that govern growth and pattern formation.

Professor Satyajit Mayor
Membrane organisation and living interfaces

Studies how cells build functional membrane architecture in space and time, and how membrane composition and mechanics influence signalling, endocytosis, and cellular decision-making.

Dr Darius Köster
Membrane-cortex mechanics and tension regulation

Focuses on the molecular and physical principles that link the plasma membrane, cell cortex, and cytoskeletal forces, with quantitative reconstitution and imaging of mechano-sensing processes.

Dr Falk Schneider
Fluorescence imaging of membrane dynamics

Develops advanced fluorescence microscopy and fluctuation spectroscopy tools to measure nanoscale molecular motion and membrane organisation across model membranes, cells, and zebrafish tissues.

Dr Eva Kreysing
Neuronal mechanobiology and tissue stiffness sensing

Examines how neurons sense the stiffness of their environment, using surface plasmon resonance microscopy and biomechanics to connect tissue mechanics with neuronal maturation.

Dr Amit Singh
Bioelectric morphogenesis and multiscale modeling

Uses physics-informed mathematical modeling to explore how bioelectric and mechanical signals coordinate tissue growth, patterning, and morphogenesis in developing and regenerative systems.
